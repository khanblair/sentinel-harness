# What the briefs say

## What it is

A brief is a short text that Sentinel builds from the facts of your project. It uses no agent, so it costs nothing.

## Steps

1. The Morning brief says what is waiting today: open tasks in the backlog, tasks left Blocked or Partial by the last run and why, and approvals, decisions, and Brain proposals waiting. It says what you have: the spend this week against the cap, the agents that are ready, and the host state. It ends with the first thing to do.
2. The Sundown wrap-up says what Sentinel did today: how many tasks were verified, partial, blocked, and skipped, and what was spent. It says what is stuck and why, what waits for you, and what to start tomorrow. It ends with one honest line of status.
3. In Telegram, send `/brief` for the Morning brief and `/wrapup` for the Sundown wrap-up. `/daily` is a shorter summary of today: tasks verified, spend, and the run state.
4. The last 14 briefs are kept in Settings, Routines.
