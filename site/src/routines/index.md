# Routines

## What it is

A routine is a job that runs at a set time, like a cron job but set up in plain words. Two are built in: the Morning brief gets you ready for the day, and the Sundown wrap-up tells you how the day went. You can also add reminders and a timed backlog run. The results show in the app and can arrive in Telegram.

## Steps

1. Open Settings, then Routines. You can also use the Routines row under Scheduled in the sidebar.
2. Turn on the Morning brief or the Sundown wrap-up. Pick the time and the days. The times use this computer's clock.
3. Leave "Send to Telegram" on to get the brief in your chat. Your Telegram chat needs the Reports level.
4. Press Send now to see a brief at once.
5. Press Add a reminder to set your own text at a time, for example "Review the plan before standup".

## Tip

Sentinel has to be running at the time. On an always-on machine that is all day. If it was off at the time and is under 2 hours late, the routine still runs when Sentinel starts.
