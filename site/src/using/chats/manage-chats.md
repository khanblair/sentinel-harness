# Rename, pin, archive, and delete

## What it is

Each chat has a menu with the actions for that chat. Rename and pin keep a chat easy to find. Archive hides a chat you are done with. Delete removes a chat's messages and uploads.

## Steps

1. Open the chat menu. Click the three dots on the chat's row in the sidebar. On a phone, long-press the row. The chat title at the top of the stream opens the same menu, plus Export report.
2. Rename. Choose Rename, and edit the title in place.
3. Pin. Choose Pin to keep the chat at the top of its project.
4. Archive. Choose Archive to hide the chat in the Archived group at the bottom of the sidebar. Open that group and choose Unarchive to bring the chat back.
5. Delete. Choose Delete. Sentinel asks first. Delete removes the chat's messages and uploads. It never touches the repository or its commits.
6. Search. Click the search button at the top of the sidebar to filter chats by title. Search covers every project.
7. A chat with a live run cannot be archived or deleted. The live run still writes to this chat and to the project's working tree. Stop the run first, then archive or delete the chat.
