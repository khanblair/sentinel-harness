# Panels

## What it is

Panels open as tabs beside the stream. The top bar has icons for the main panels, and the More menu holds the rest.

## Steps

1. Click a top bar icon to open a panel: Board, Terminal, Changes, Reserved, Brain, or Remote. Board shows tasks by status.
2. Open the More menu for Files, Cost, Report, Agents, and Permissions.
3. Expand a panel to make it wider, or close it from its tab.
4. On a tablet or phone, panels slide over the stream.
5. On a phone, the bottom tabs are Stream, Board, Reserved, Changes, and More. The More tab holds the other panels.

Each top bar icon has a tooltip and a name, so you can find a panel by hovering over its icon.
