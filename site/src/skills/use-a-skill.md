# Use a skill by hand

## What it is

You can add any skill to your next message yourself, even one that is switched Off. This is useful when you want one skill for one job.

## Steps

1. Open a chat in your project.
2. Press the plus button in the composer and choose Use a skill. Or type `/skill` and the name, for example `/skill release-checklist`.
3. The skill appears as a chip above the text box. Press the x on the chip to remove it.
4. Send your message. The skill goes into the task prompts of that run.

## Screenshot

![Screenshot of a skill chip in the composer](../images/skill-chip.png)

## Tip

A skill only adds words to the prompt. It cannot change which files a task may write, the budget, or the permission mode.
