# Guided tour

## What it is

The guided tour points at seven parts of the run screen, one stop at a time, with a sample run to point at.

## Steps

1. Open the tour from the prototype toolbar. You can replay it later from Settings.
2. Move to the next stop with the keyboard, or with a swipe on a touch screen.
3. Read each stop in order:
   - The run list.
   - The agent switcher.
   - The panel icons.
   - The stream.
   - The permission chip.
   - The send-to chip.
   - Settings.
