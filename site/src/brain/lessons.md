# Lessons

## What it is

A lesson is a note with typed fields that records a fact from past runs. Sentinel trusts a lesson only after it helps.

## Steps

1. A new lesson starts as a candidate.
2. After two helpful uses and no harm, a candidate becomes proven.
3. A harmful use makes a lesson rejected.
4. A lesson that goes unused for a long time becomes retired.
5. Review lessons in the Brain panel, or from your phone. Approve, reject, or retire each one.

## Tip

A lesson holds typed fields only. Task titles and backlog text never enter a lesson note.
